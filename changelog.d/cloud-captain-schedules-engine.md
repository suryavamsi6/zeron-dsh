Cloud-hosted Captains' schedules now land in the edge store: on a session machine, `schedule_create`/`edit`/`delete` for the hosted chat forward over the runner channel, and `schedule_list` merges the edge rows with the local store, so a schedule survives the machine sleeping and fires edge-side. Fired occurrences arrive as durable wake-up rows the machine pulls, materializes as ordinary queued chat messages, and acknowledges — with edge-side retry covering a missed pull. Desktop Captains are unchanged: their schedules stay in `schedules.json` (SUR-269).
